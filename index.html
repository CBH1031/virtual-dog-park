Dog Encyclopedia

Three breeds, three very different personalities.

Beagle

Size
Small to medium — about 33–40 cm (13–15 in) tall and 9–14 kg (20–30 lb).
Personality
Friendly, cheerful and endlessly curious. A beagle follows its nose everywhere and hates being left alone.
Everyday care
Two good walks a day, plus a safe garden to explore. Brush once a week and check inside those long ears.

Golden Retriever

Size
Large — about 55–61 cm (22–24 in) tall and 25–34 kg (55–75 lb).
Personality
Gentle, patient and eager to please. Wonderful with children, and happiest carrying something in its mouth.
Everyday care
Plenty of exercise and play every day. Brush two or three times a week — daily while it is shedding.

Poodle

Size
Three sizes: Toy, Miniature and Standard. A Standard is about 45–60 cm (18–24 in) tall and 20–32 kg (45–70 lb).
Personality
Very clever and playful, which makes a poodle quick to learn — and quick to get bored.
Everyday care
Brush every day or two. The curly coat never stops growing, so it needs a trim every 4–6 weeks.
